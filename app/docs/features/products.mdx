# Products

Learn about our product offerings and how to manage them effectively.

## Product Overview

Products are the core entities in our system. They represent the items or services you offer to your customers.

## Creating Products

To create a new product:

1. Navigate to the Products section
2. Click "Create New Product"
3. Fill in the required information
4. Save your product

## Product Types

We support various product types:

- **Physical Products**: Tangible items that require shipping
- **Digital Products**: Downloadable content or services
- **Subscription Products**: Recurring billing products
- **Service Products**: One-time or ongoing services

## Product Configuration

Each product can be configured with:

- Pricing information
- Inventory settings
- Tax configurations
- Custom attributes

## Best Practices

- Use clear, descriptive product names
- Include detailed descriptions
- Set appropriate pricing tiers
- Configure inventory tracking for physical products
